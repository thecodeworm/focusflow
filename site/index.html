One calm window for the whole session.

Timer, music, tasks and statistics sit in a dashboard you can close at any time. The floating timer stays.

Stays in view. Stays out of the way.

The floating timer sits on top of every window, and on macOS it floats over full-screen apps too. Clicking it never pulls you out of what you’re doing.

Pick the size that suits the moment: just the time, the ring with controls, or the ring with your task, phase and music.

Keeps time, even while your laptop sleeps.

The countdown lives in a small native core, not in a browser tab. Every window only shows what that one clock says, so the floating timer, the dashboard and the menu bar always agree.

  • Close the lid mid-session and it still ends on time. Prefer it to pause while asleep? That’s one setting.
  • Changing the system clock or time zone never touches a running session.
  • Quit or restart, and Ringlet picks up where it left off.
  • Only finished sessions count. Reset or skipped ones are never recorded.

Music without another tab.

Paste a YouTube video, live stream or playlist, or a direct link to an audio file. It plays right inside Ringlet through YouTube’s official player, and you can pause it from the floating timer.

Or skip the music. White, pink and brown noise are generated on your computer, work offline, and mix with whatever else is playing.

See where the hours went.

Choose a task before you start, and each finished session counts toward it. Your week appears as a quiet bar chart, along with today’s focus time, your streak, and time spent on each task.

Opening it the first time

Ringlet isn’t code-signed yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Open the disk image and drag Ringlet into Applications.
  2. Open Ringlet. When macOS says it can’t check the app, choose Done.
  3. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

After that it opens like any other app.

Opening it the first time

Ringlet isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks you to confirm it once.

  1. Run the installer you downloaded.
  2. If you see Windows protected your PC, click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway and finish the installer.

If the WebView2 runtime is missing, the installer downloads it for you.
